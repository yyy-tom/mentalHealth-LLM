# Anxiety: Understanding and Managing

## What is Anxiety?

Anxiety is a natural response to stress or perceived threats. While some anxiety is normal and even helpful, excessive or persistent anxiety can interfere with daily life.

## Common Symptoms

- Racing heart or palpitations
- Difficulty breathing or shortness of breath
- Muscle tension
- Restlessness or feeling on edge
- Difficulty concentrating
- Sleep problems
- Excessive worry about everyday matters

## Evidence-Based Coping Strategies

### 1. Deep Breathing (4-7-8 Technique)
- Breathe in through your nose for 4 seconds
- Hold your breath for 7 seconds
- Exhale slowly through your mouth for 8 seconds
- Repeat 3-4 times

### 2. Grounding Techniques (5-4-3-2-1)
When feeling overwhelmed:
- Name 5 things you can SEE
- Name 4 things you can TOUCH
- Name 3 things you can HEAR
- Name 2 things you can SMELL
- Name 1 thing you can TASTE

### 3. Progressive Muscle Relaxation
Systematically tense and relax muscle groups, starting from your toes and working up to your head.

### 4. Cognitive Restructuring
Challenge anxious thoughts by asking:
- What evidence supports this worry?
- What evidence contradicts it?
- What would I tell a friend in this situation?

## When to Seek Professional Help

Consider professional support if:
- Anxiety significantly impacts work, relationships, or daily activities
- Physical symptoms are severe or frequent
- You're using alcohol or substances to cope
- You experience panic attacks
- Anxiety has lasted more than 6 months

## Self-Care Foundations

- Regular physical activity (30 minutes most days)
- Consistent sleep schedule (7-9 hours)
- Limit caffeine and alcohol
- Practice mindfulness or meditation
- Maintain social connections
