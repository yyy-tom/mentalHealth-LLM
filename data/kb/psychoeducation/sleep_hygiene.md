# Sleep Hygiene: Better Sleep for Better Mental Health

## Why Sleep Matters

Quality sleep is essential for mental health. Poor sleep can:
- Worsen anxiety and depression
- Impair concentration and memory
- Increase irritability
- Lower stress tolerance

## Sleep Hygiene Guidelines

### 1. Consistent Schedule
- Go to bed and wake up at the same time daily
- Even on weekends, stay within 1 hour of your usual times
- Avoid sleeping in to "catch up"

### 2. Create a Sleep-Friendly Environment
- Keep your bedroom dark, quiet, and cool (65-68°F / 18-20°C)
- Use your bed only for sleep (and intimacy)
- Remove electronic devices from the bedroom

### 3. Wind-Down Routine (30-60 minutes before bed)
- Dim the lights
- Avoid screens (or use blue light filters)
- Try relaxing activities: reading, gentle stretching, warm bath
- Practice deep breathing or meditation

### 4. Watch What You Consume
- Avoid caffeine 6+ hours before bed
- Limit alcohol (it disrupts sleep quality)
- Don't eat heavy meals close to bedtime
- Stay hydrated, but not right before bed

### 5. Daytime Habits Matter
- Get natural light exposure in the morning
- Exercise regularly (but not within 3 hours of bedtime)
- Limit naps to 20-30 minutes, before 3pm

## When You Can't Sleep

- If you're awake for 20+ minutes, get up
- Do something calm in dim light (reading, gentle stretching)
- Return to bed only when sleepy
- Don't watch the clock

## Common Sleep Disruptors

| Problem | Solution |
|---------|----------|
| Racing thoughts | Write them down before bed |
| Anxiety | Practice relaxation techniques |
| Irregular schedule | Set alarms for both sleep and wake |
| Screen time | Use night mode, stop 1 hour before bed |
| Uncomfortable bed | Invest in quality mattress/pillows |

## When to Seek Help

Consider professional help if:
- Sleep problems persist for more than 3 weeks
- You experience excessive daytime sleepiness
- You snore loudly or stop breathing during sleep
- Sleep issues significantly impact daily functioning

Sleep disorders are treatable. Don't suffer in silence.
