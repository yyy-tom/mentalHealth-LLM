# Coping Strategies: Evidence-Based Techniques

## Breathing Exercises

### Box Breathing (4-4-4-4)
1. Breathe in for 4 seconds
2. Hold for 4 seconds
3. Breathe out for 4 seconds
4. Hold for 4 seconds
5. Repeat 4 times

### 4-7-8 Breathing
1. Inhale through nose for 4 seconds
2. Hold breath for 7 seconds
3. Exhale through mouth for 8 seconds
4. Repeat 3-4 times

### Diaphragmatic Breathing
1. Place one hand on chest, one on belly
2. Breathe so your belly rises (not chest)
3. Exhale slowly
4. Practice for 5-10 minutes daily

## Grounding Techniques

### 5-4-3-2-1 Sensory Grounding
When feeling overwhelmed or anxious:
- **5** things you can SEE
- **4** things you can TOUCH
- **3** things you can HEAR
- **2** things you can SMELL
- **1** thing you can TASTE

### Body Scan
1. Sit or lie comfortably
2. Focus attention on your toes
3. Notice sensations without judgment
4. Slowly move attention up through your body
5. End at the top of your head

### Temperature Reset
- Hold ice cubes in your hands
- Splash cold water on your face
- This activates the dive reflex and calms the nervous system

## Cognitive Techniques

### Thought Challenging
Ask yourself:
1. What evidence supports this thought?
2. What evidence contradicts it?
3. What would I tell a friend thinking this?
4. What's a more balanced perspective?

### Worry Time
1. Set aside 15-20 minutes daily
2. Write down all worries during this time
3. Outside worry time, tell yourself "I'll think about that later"
4. Over time, worries often feel less urgent

### Defusion
Instead of "I'm a failure," try:
- "I'm having the thought that I'm a failure"
- Acknowledge thoughts without believing them

## Behavioral Strategies

### Behavioral Activation
When depressed:
1. List activities that used to bring joy
2. Schedule one small activity daily
3. Notice any positive feelings
4. Gradually increase activities

### Pleasant Events Scheduling
- Plan at least one enjoyable activity each day
- Mix of social, physical, and creative activities
- Start small and build up

### Problem-Solving Steps
1. Define the problem clearly
2. Brainstorm solutions (no judging yet)
3. Evaluate pros and cons
4. Choose one approach
5. Try it and review results

## Self-Compassion Practices

### Self-Compassion Break
1. Acknowledge: "This is a moment of suffering"
2. Remember: "Suffering is part of being human"
3. Offer kindness: "May I be kind to myself"

### Treat Yourself Like a Friend
- What would you say to a friend in this situation?
- Use that same tone and words with yourself

## Quick Stress Relief

| Time Available | Technique |
|---------------|-----------|
| 30 seconds | 3 deep breaths |
| 2 minutes | 5-4-3-2-1 grounding |
| 5 minutes | Progressive muscle relaxation |
| 10 minutes | Brief meditation or walk |
| 20 minutes | Journaling or exercise |

## Building a Coping Toolkit

Create your personal toolkit:
1. Identify your warning signs
2. List techniques that work for YOU
3. Practice when you're calm
4. Have your list accessible
5. Be patient with yourself
